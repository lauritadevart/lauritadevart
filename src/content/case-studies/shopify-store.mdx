## My role

I handled the full build — choosing and customizing the theme, designing the pages, loading the product catalog, setting up collections, payments, and shipping, and configuring SEO and analytics.

## Outcome

A polished, mobile-first Shopify store that was live and taking orders quickly, with everything the client needed to manage it going forward.

## How I approached it

### Brand-led theme customization

Rather than fight a stock theme, I customized it to match the client's colors, typography, and personality so the store feels like their brand.

> People trust a store that looks considered. The details add up to credibility.

### Built for mobile first

Most shoppers browse on their phones, so I designed and tested the whole experience mobile-first, then scaled up.

### Ready to grow

I set up collections, product variants, and analytics so the client can track performance and expand their catalog with confidence.

## What shipped

A complete Shopify store with a customized theme, product catalog, secure payments, shipping configuration, SEO setup, and a walkthrough so the client can manage orders and products independently.
