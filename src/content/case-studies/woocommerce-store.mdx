End to end — building the WooCommerce store, customizing the theme, setting up product catalogs, configuring payments and shipping, and applying on-page SEO. Delivered as a handoff the client's team could run without me.

## How I approached it

### Performance first

Slow stores lose sales. I optimized hosting, compressed images, and cleaned up plugins so pages load fast on any device.

> A store that loads in under three seconds converts far better than one that takes ten.

### A catalog the client can manage

I set up clear product categories, attributes, and a simple editing workflow so the team can add products and update stock without touching code.

### Checkout that gets out of the way

I streamlined the checkout to remove friction and reduce cart abandonment, testing every step on mobile and desktop.

## What shipped

A complete WooCommerce store with product catalogs, secure payment and shipping setup, on-page SEO, an optimized checkout, and documentation so the client's team can run it independently.
