## My role

I rebuilt the site in WordPress with Elementor, restructured the content, and applied technical and on-page SEO — from metadata and headings to site speed and internal linking.

## Outcome

A fast, professional website with a clear structure that search engines can understand and the client's team can update easily.

## How I approached it

### Structure before decoration

I mapped the pages and content hierarchy first, so the site is logical for both visitors and search engines.

> Good SEO starts with a clear structure, not a pile of keywords.

### Technical SEO done properly

I fixed site speed, added proper headings and metadata, optimized images, and set up internal linking to help pages rank.

### Easy to maintain

By building with Elementor and clean WordPress fundamentals, the client can update content without breaking the design.

## What shipped

A complete WordPress site built with Elementor, fully responsive, with technical and on-page SEO applied throughout, plus documentation for ongoing maintenance.
