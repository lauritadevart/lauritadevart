## 4.2 Total cost

The total cost of a project represents the sum of all the economic resources needed to carry it out, including both direct and indirect expenses.

CT = CD + CI (ec. 4.1)

Where:
- CT: total cost
- CD: direct costs
- CI: indirect costs

CD = SB + SC + SS + MD + DP + OG (ec. 4.2)

Where:
- SB: basic salaries
- SC: complementary salaries (payment to the vacation fund)
- SS: social security
- MD: direct materials
- DP: per diems and travel expenses.
- OG: Other Expenses.

### 4.2.1 Calculating SB

The total basic salary (SB) represents the base remuneration assigned to the students for the time invested in the project, excluding additional benefits such as vacations or social security contributions. This value includes the sum of the base salaries of all the people who participate in the preparation of the project proposal.

SB = Σ(i=1..n) Ai · Bi (ec. 4.3)

Where:
- Ai: days devoted to the research by participants.
- Bi: daily salary of the participant (monthly salary / 24).
- N: total number of participants.

B1 = 400 CUP / 24 = 16.667 CUP

SB = (56 days × 16.667 CUP) + (30 days × 281.991 CUP) + (30 days × 187.5 CUP) + (30 days × 231.667 CUP)
SB = 21968 CUP

### 4.2.2 Calculating SC

The complementary salary (SC) equals 9.09% of the basic salary and is intended to cover vacation pay, according to the standards and regulations established at the national level.

SC = 0.0909 × SB (ec. 4.4)
SC = 0.0909 × 21968 CUP
SC = 1996.899 CUP

### 4.2.3 Calculating SS

Social security equals 10% of the basic salary including the complementary salary.

SS = 0.05 × (SB + SC) (ec. 4.5)
SS = 0.05 × (21968 CUP + 1996.899 CUP)
SS = 1198.245 CUP

### 4.2.8 Calculating CI

This refers to the general expenses indispensable for the execution of the project, but which are not directly linked to a particular activity, such as electricity consumption, supervision costs, or the use of infrastructure. These expenses are calculated by applying a coefficient to the base salary of the research project.

CI = 0.84 × SB (ec. 4.5)

## 4.4 Project impact

### 4.4.1 Economic impact

The modernization of the rolling mill at the Antillana de Acero Company will have a transcendental economic impact, since it increases the efficiency and productive capacity of the company, reducing operating costs and improving product quality. This enables the company to compete in more demanding markets, increasing its revenue and strengthening its position in the steel industry. In addition, investment in modern technology and equipment generates a positive effect on the supply chain, benefiting suppliers and customers. At the macroeconomic level, the project contributes to the growth of the industrial sector, generating direct and indirect jobs and promoting the economic development of the region. Altogether, this modernization not only boosts the company's profitability, but also strengthens the local and national economy.

### 4.4.2 Social impact

The modernization of the rolling mill at the Antillana de Acero Company will have a significant social impact, since it not only improves the working conditions of the workers, but also promotes training in new technologies. By implementing more efficient and safer processes, a healthier and more productive work environment is promoted, which directly benefits employees and their families. In addition, by strengthening the competitiveness of the company, the economic development of the community is contributed to, creating a multiplier effect in the region. This project reinforces the company's commitment to social well-being and people's progress, consolidating its role as a key actor in local development.

### 4.4.3 Environmental impact

The modernization of the rolling mill at the Antillana de Acero Company will represent an opportunity to reduce the environmental impact of its operations. By incorporating more efficient and sustainable technologies, energy consumption will be decreased, contributing to a cleaner production process. In addition, the project includes compliance with environmental regulations, ensuring that the exploitation of resources is carried out responsibly. With these improvements, the company will not only optimize its production, but will also reinforce its commitment to environmental protection and sustainable development.

## 4.5 Partial conclusions

The economic study carried out in this project demonstrated its viability, concluding that the company will obtain benefits if it decides to implement it, since it will ensure greater stability in the production of steel profiles in the rolling mill. This advancement will not only drive an increase in the company's income, but will also have a positive impact on the country's economy.
