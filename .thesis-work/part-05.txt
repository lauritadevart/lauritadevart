# Chapter 3: Monitoring interface and communication network

## 3.1 Introduction

This chapter explores the features of WinCC software, developed by Siemens, which enables real-time visualization through customizable graphical interfaces, integrating data from PLCs and field devices via protocols such as PROFINET and PROFIBUS. Likewise, it includes advanced tools for alarm management, historical data collection, report generation, and access control, guaranteeing safe and efficient operations. In this context, WinCC V7.5 is used to supervise and control a 300 rolling mill, although it is stressed that the current system does not fully comply with the functionalities of a SCADA, mainly due to the absence of a real-time fault diagnosis and correction algorithm. In addition, the incorporation of new screens and monitoring variables to manage the auxiliary equipment located before and after line 320 is detailed below. A proposal to optimize the communication network through a client-server architecture, using PROFIBUS and PROFINET networks in order to increase the speed and flexibility of the connections, is also presented. Lastly, the concept of the automation pyramid is introduced, which represents the hierarchical levels of an industrial system, ranging from sensors and actuators at the field level to corporate management at the enterprise level.

## 3.2 Software features

WinCC, developed by Siemens, is a robust and flexible SCADA software used to create monitoring and control systems in industrial environments. It allows real-time visualization of processes through customizable graphical interfaces, integrating data from PLCs and field devices via protocols such as PROFINET, PROFIBUS, and OPC UA. WinCC offers advanced tools for alarm management, historical data acquisition, report generation, and access control, ensuring safe and efficient operations. Its scalability makes it ideal for applications in manufacturing, energy, water, oil & gas, and smart buildings, providing a reliable solution to optimize processes and improve industrial productivity. In this case we will use WinCC V7.5.

## 3.3 Screen description

In the center there is an existing monitoring system where the entire 300 rolling mill is attended with real-time values and is capable of controlling them. However, it cannot be considered a SCADA system due to the lack of a fault diagnosis and identification algorithm and of real-time correction actions. In this way it will be simply referenced as a monitoring system.

In the existing monitoring software there was no screen dedicated to the processes before and after line 320; therefore, as part of the objectives, monitoring variables and two screens were added for the control of the auxiliary equipment. These variables fulfill specific functions that are clarified in the flowcharts.

## 3.4 Communication network

Communication on line 320 operates using the SNP-X protocol, which has limitations for data exchange. The lack of compatibility between the GE Fanuc PLCs and WinCC made it necessary to resort to the OPC standard as a solution to facilitate communication.

In the proposal, improving this communication was considered by establishing a client-server architecture. For this, it will be necessary to propose an HP EliteDesk 800 G6 Tower PC (see Annex 4), which is a robust and versatile equipment that offers excellent performance for server applications in controlled environments. It is equipped with 10th-generation Intel Core i5 processors, which guarantees high processing power to handle demanding tasks. Regarding RAM memory, it supports up to 64 GB DDR4, allowing efficient handling of data and real-time applications. In terms of storage, it has a configurable SSD that can range from 256 GB to 1 TB, providing speed and capacity for the operating system and critical applications. In addition, it offers the option to add a 1 TB or larger HDD, ideal for historical data storage and backups. The integrated network card is Gigabit Ethernet, ensuring a fast and reliable connection for network communication. The equipment is compatible with operating systems such as Windows 10 Pro or Windows Server, which makes it suitable for enterprise and industrial environments. Regarding connectivity, it includes multiple USB ports (including USB-C), HDMI and DisplayPort, in addition to allowing the installation of additional cards for industrial protocols such as RS-232/485 or PROFINET. Its cooling design is efficient and it features quiet fans, which contributes to stable and prolonged operation. In terms of security, it incorporates TPM 2.0 for data encryption and advanced protection features, guaranteeing the integrity of the information. Although it is not specifically designed for extreme industrial environments, its robust construction makes it adequate to operate in controlled conditions, offering an ideal balance between performance, reliability, and cost.

The PCs and the PLCs will be connected by a PROFIBUS network to increase transmission speed and make the connections more versatile, while the PLCs will be connected to the different actuators by PROFIBUS because that is the technology these devices support. In this way, having the remote stations will represent a saving in modules and components and will be much more durable than the simple connection that existed before. Internally, communication between the modules and the PLC CPU is managed through the SIEMENS hardware's internal bus.

[Figure 3.4 Automation pyramid.]

PROFIBUS (Process Field Bus) is a fieldbus standard widely used in industrial automation. Based on RS-485 or optical fiber, it allows fast and reliable communication between devices such as PLCs, sensors, and actuators. It works in a master-slave topology and stands out for its robustness in harsh industrial environments. It is used in discrete manufacturing and continuous process applications. PROFINET is the Ethernet-based industrial communication protocol for Industry 4.0. It offers real-time communication (RT/IRT), integration with IT networks, and high speed (up to 1 Gbps). It supports plug-and-play configuration and advanced diagnostics, making it ideal for complex systems such as robotics or automated production lines. It is the modern evolution of PROFIBUS, adapted to the needs of digitalization.

The automation pyramid represents the hierarchical levels of an industrial system:

- Field: sensors/actuators.
- Control: PLCs.
- Supervision: SCADA, HMI, and monitoring.
- Planning: MES and production management.
- Enterprise: ERP and corporate management.

Each level communicates vertically, optimizing processes from plant operation to strategic decision-making. In the center, the last 2 levels are truncated; it would be important to propose their integration in future projects.

## 3.5 Partial conclusions

In conclusion, the use of WinCC software stands out as an essential tool for supervision and control in industrial environments, offering a scalable and reliable solution to optimize processes and improve productivity. Although the current monitoring system of the 300 rolling mill does not fulfill all the functions of a SCADA, new screens and monitoring variables were added, representing a significant advance in the improvement of the control of the auxiliary equipment before and after line 320. It was proposed to improve the communication network through a client-server architecture and the use of PROFIBUS and PROFINET networks to increase the efficiency and durability of the system, reducing costs and improving data transmission speed. In addition, the automation pyramid presented in the chapter underlines the importance of an efficient hierarchical integration of the different levels of an industrial system.
