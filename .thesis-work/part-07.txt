# General conclusions

In this work, a study was carried out on the automation and electrical drive systems applied to the process. The knowledge gathered during the research served as the basis for the design of a proposal for an automation and electrical drive system in the subprocesses prior to line 320 of the 300 rolling mill at Antillana de Acero. From this analysis it can be concluded that, once the technical automation means, the control strategy, and the monitoring system have been selected, it is feasible to design an automation and electrical drive system for the auxiliary equipment of line 320 of the 300 rolling mill at Antillana de Acero. The proposed system outperforms the current one, since replacing the existing controllers with a more modern one and implementing more advanced monitoring software solves problems such as frequent failures, lack of spare parts, the limitations of the SNP-X protocol, and the restrictions of the software used to program the PLCs. In addition, the proposal to include a second PC as a server guarantees communication superior to the one existing in the current system, considering that a communication network was also designed to integrate the subprocesses into the monitoring level within the automation pyramid. The proposed system outperforms the current one from the energetic and ecological point of view, optimizing the use of resources and reducing the environmental impact. Finally, the project is economically viable, which reinforces its feasibility of implementation.

# Recommendations

To extend the results obtained through the study carried out and to optimize their use, it is recommended:

- To carry out the corresponding programming of the PLCs to be used, following the line of the flowcharts presented and implementing the algorithms of those equipments not covered in this work.
- To implement, either in the existing monitoring software or in a completely new one, a fault detection system and even a self-regulating system, which would mean a great technological advancement for the rolling mill and the company in general.
